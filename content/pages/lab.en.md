title: The Lab
---
Blue is the ink. Yolk is the life. Together they make something new.

Blue Yolk began as a quiet, exclusive workplace for thinking, making and testing.

We want it to become an open-source creative lab: a place where methods, tools and work are shared, and where people from any field can come to make something that does not fit neatly into one category.

There is no physical space yet. For now the lab is this site, and the people who work in it.
